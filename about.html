Our Story

Konnecting Brands to Digital Excellence Since 2018

UrbanKonnect was founded in 2018 with a simple yet powerful vision: to help businesses in Dehradun and across Uttarakhand harness the power of digital marketing to achieve extraordinary growth.

What started as a small team of three passionate digital marketers in a modest office near Clock Tower has grown into Dehradun's most trusted digital marketing agency, serving over 200 clients across various industries. Our journey has been fueled by an unwavering commitment to delivering measurable results and building lasting partnerships.

We recognized early on that businesses in Uttarakhand needed more than just generic marketing solutions—they needed strategies tailored to the local market while leveraging global best practices. This insight became the cornerstone of our approach, helping us deliver campaigns that consistently exceed expectations.

Today, as we celebrate over 500 successful projects and maintain a 98% client retention rate, we remain as committed as ever to our founding principle: authentic connections between brands and their audiences drive real business growth.

Our Mission

To empower businesses in Uttarakhand and beyond with innovative, data-driven digital marketing strategies that deliver measurable growth. We strive to make world-class digital marketing accessible to businesses of all sizes, from local startups to established enterprises.

Through transparency, creativity, and relentless focus on results, we help our clients navigate the digital landscape with confidence and achieve their business objectives faster than they thought possible.

Our Vision

To be recognized as North India's most innovative and results-oriented digital marketing agency by 2027, known for transforming businesses through strategic digital excellence and building a legacy of successful partnerships.

We envision a future where every business in Uttarakhand has access to cutting-edge digital marketing expertise, enabling them to compete effectively in the global marketplace while staying rooted in local values.
